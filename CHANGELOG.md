# Changelog

## 2.0.0

Server owners: the economy files change in this update (the Garden Guide's type, ginseng's lifetime, the opium poppy's, coca's and cannabis's seed packets and classes, the peeled sweet potato and the joints), so on the first start the server rewrites `gebsplants-types.xml`, `gebsplants-events-children.xml` and `gebsplants-cfgeconomycore.xml` in `$profile:Gebs/mpmissions/`. Copy the new `gebsplants-types.xml` into `mpmissions/<your mission>/gebsplants/` again, and redo any edits you made to the old files.

### Added
- **Opium poppy**, the 28th crop: sow its seeds (from Opium Poppy Seed Packets, farm loot like the others) in a garden plot and it grows a clump of tall, waxy grey-green stems that flower lilac with a dark purple heart, then ripen three blue-grey seed pods to harvest. The pods aren't food and have no use yet; they're there to be grown and gathered. The pod is a real opium poppy's, its crown of stigma rays included.
- **Coca**, the 29th crop: sow its seeds (from Coca Seed Packets, farm loot like the others) in a garden plot and it grows into a woody bush, reddish-brown stems and side twigs crowded with small, glossy oval leaves, with green berries that ripen red. Harvest three handfuls of fresh leaves. Like the poppy pods they aren't food and have no use yet.
- **Cannabis**, the 30th crop: sow its seeds (from Cannabis Seed Packets, farm loot like the others) in a garden plot and it grows one tall stalk with pairs of big, deeply cut fan leaves and side branches, then flowers with sticky buds at every tip. A ripe plant gives three buds of 5 g, which stack in grams. The plant is built from vanilla's own cannabis photos, and the bud from real photos of a cured nug and a frosty close-up: dense calyx clusters, orange hairs and sugar leaves.
- **Joints**: combine a bud with a sheet of paper to roll a joint (it takes a gram), then combine the joint with a lighter or a box of matches to light it; its tip glows. Hold the lit joint and hold left click to smoke it, with vanilla's eating animation: six puffs to a joint, and now and then a cough. Every puff tints the world round the colours of the rainbow for two and a half minutes more, up to 25 minutes (about 15 for a whole joint), and the first few puffs make it stronger. It comes on over a few seconds and fades out over its last minute. Only the smoker sees it; a mask gets in the way, as with eating; dying ends it, and it doesn't carry over a logout.
- **Sweet potatoes are peeled before they're eaten or cooked**, as vanilla's potatoes are: peel a raw sweet potato with a blade for its bright orange flesh. A whole, unpeeled one can't be eaten or cooked any more (cut it into seed pieces or peel it).
- **Geb's Garden Guide**, vanilla's folded map retextured as a field guide: its own cover, and a poster of all 30 crops (ripening time and harvest per plant) with how-to-grow notes. Rare loot on the shelves of farms, villages and towns. It opens the way vanilla's map does: on servers with the 2D map as a page on the screen (scroll to zoom, drag to move), on servers with the 3D map in your hands.
- **Every crop looks like vanilla's garden plants, from real photos.** All 30 plants are built the way vanilla builds its tomatoes, zucchini and potatoes: every leaf is a photo cut-out on a light card, with vanilla's swaying leaf materials, relief and shine. Leaves and harvests come from free photos of the real crops (and vanilla's own leaf and food photos where they fit): an ear of corn with real kernel rows, curved husk leaves wrapped round it and peeled back, and a soft tuft of silk; onion and garlic skins; bean, pea and okra pods; peanut shells, strawberries and hop cones; carrots, beets, turnips, radishes, sweet potatoes, leeks, cabbages, melons and squash. Every stage of the harvest is cooked from those photos: raw, baked, boiled, dried, burnt and rotten. Cooked strawberries, onions, garlic and radishes wrinkle and shrivel like vanilla's cooked tomato and pepper. Burnt food is charred like vanilla's: mottled black and brown char over the food's own cooked colour, grey ash on the raised parts, blistered relief and a carbon sheen, instead of a flat black. The sunflower head, the leek and the radish bunch (now with its leafy tops) are rebuilt from real photos too. Ripe plants carry their crop, as vanilla's zucchini plant does, and wild ginseng's root and leaves are redone the same way.

### Changed
- Partial tobacco bunches and hands can be combined like other stackable items, keeping green and cured tobacco separate.
- Tying a tobacco hand preserves the amount of leaves supplied. Add matching leaves to top it up to 300 g.
- Bales accept partial cured hands and stop at five full hands, leaving excess tobacco in the source hand. Existing saved bales retain their fill level. Condition is weighted by the amount transferred.
- **Corn and tobacco fit in a polytunnel.** In vanilla's polytunnel garden, ripe corn reached 0.6 m through the plastic over the side rows and tobacco's lower leaves reached out through the wall. Corn now grows to 1.8 m (a 1.55 m stalk and shorter leaves) and tobacco holds its leaves higher and closer (the lower ones about half a metre long), so both stay inside on all 13 slots at every stage. Hop poles are 2.1 m (were 2.3 m), clear of the roof. The sunflower, taller than a man, still touches the roof over the side rows.
- **Yellow squash is closer to vanilla's zucchini.** A plant's four squash gave about 2.6 times a zucchini plant's food for a similar grow. A squash now weighs 125 g (was 300 g) with the zucchini's nutrition per 100 g, so a plant gives about 1.25 times a zucchini plant's for its 22% longer grow.
- **Leeks take 3x4 inventory slots** (were 1x4), which fits their 43 x 60 cm leaf fan at vanilla's scale, as the sunflower head's 43 cm takes three.
- **Plants only gently stir in the wind.** The leaves and stems of growing plants move about a third as much as vanilla's leaf materials make them, and seedlings, stakes and poles (with the bines wound round them) and harvests sitting on the soil (bulbs, roots, berries, melon stalks) don't sway at all.

### Fixed
- **Inventory pictures show the whole crop.** Every harvest's inventory camera looked at it end-on, so a cucumber, a carrot or a leek showed as a small disc. They're now seen from the side, as vanilla's zucchini and potato are, or from above for leaves, bunches and pods and the tobacco hand and bale, and turned so the long side fills the item's slots. The tobacco bale's camera also sat inside the bale.
- **Crops sit properly in the hand.** Each harvest now borrows the hand pose of the vanilla food closest to it in size and shape, not the one closest in name, and sits where that food's grip closes: okra and bean pods lie in the fist like vanilla's banana instead of floating beside an open hand, turnips and beets sit in the palm on the potato's grip instead of hanging below half-open fingers, and bunches of leaves (tobacco, a tobacco hand, collards, leeks, ginseng) are held by the stems and hang from the hand instead of fanning up like a hand of cards.
- **Rotten crops are worth less than fresh ones.** Every rotten crop had 25 energy and 10 water, more than fresh radishes (20 energy) or hop cones (5) and more water than dry peanuts or grain. A rotten crop now has half its raw energy (at most 25) and 0.4 of its raw water (at most 10), always below the fresh crop, as vanilla's rotten produce is.
- **Drying no longer quadruples a crop.** Dried cucumbers, strawberries and melon slices had about four times their raw energy, where vanilla's biggest gain from drying is 2.7 times (the potato). Every dried crop is now capped at 2.7 times its raw energy.
- **Cured tobacco stays cured** when it's tied, topped up or stacked: the merge code carries the cured stage itself (it only compared decay timers, which cured fruit and veg never have), so two cured bunches always tie into a cured hand.
- **The collard plant no longer promises regrowth.** Its description said the top keeps growing once you pick the lower leaves, but DayZ's garden plants don't regrow. Reworded in all 13 languages, and on the website.
- **Burnt crops no longer gain energy.** Every crop's burnt stage carried the same 110 energy, so burning a cabbage (25) or a radish more than quadrupled it. A burnt crop now keeps its raw energy, as vanilla's burnt vegetables do, with no water and a dose of food poisoning.
- **Raw crops that should make you sick now do.** Green and pole beans, barley, sorghum, sweet potatoes and hop cones eaten raw, and green or cured tobacco, carried a toxicity value DayZ no longer uses, so they were harmless. They give food poisoning instead, mild for the grains and roots and strong for tobacco.
- **Seeds show damage.** Seeds had no damage looks, so a ruined handful looked fresh; they now darken through worn, damaged and ruined like vanilla's.
- **Crops fill the stomach like vanilla's vegetables.** Raw, baked and boiled crops had a fullness of 1.5 where vanilla's fruit and vegetables have 1, so a meal of them filled the stomach half again as fast. Every stage now matches vanilla's: 1 raw, baked, boiled and dried, 10 rotten and 5 burnt, the tobacco hand's included. Whole melons keep vanilla's whole-pumpkin values.
- **Onions come from seed packets only.** The seed-saving recipe cut seeds out of a raw onion, but onions are biennial: they set seed from a flower stalk in their second year, never in the bulb. Onion seeds and packets are unchanged.
- **Lighter models.** The growing plants had up to 17,900 faces in their first detail level (all growth stages together). None has more than 8,300 now, and each further level steps down. The last level, which DayZ also draws shadows from, is under 1,450 faces on every plant; ginseng's had 1,956, close to the ~2,000 at which binarize turns a model's shadow off. The ear of corn, the okra pod and the tobacco bale are lighter too: 7,900, 7,100 and 7,300 faces down to 5,800, 3,500 and 4,700.
- **Relief lit the right way.** Every crop's relief (normal) map had its green channel upside down, so raised veins and ridges looked pressed in under light from above. All of them are rebuilt.
- Seed packets read "Geb's Flora & Fauna" on the back; they now read "Geb's Plants".
- The config no longer carries three unused template classes (copies of vanilla's tomato plant, seed packet and seeds) inside `CfgHorticulture`, where the game never reads them.
- The seed-saving recipes have `geb_` classnames, so they can't clash with another mod's recipes.
- Baling tobacco takes any rope (vanilla's `Rope`); the extra improvised-rope line named a class that doesn't exist.
- Text: ginseng is one of the 27 garden crops, so the overview no longer reads as "27 crops and ginseng".
- **Tobacco leaves can't dead-end any more.** Green leaves fit the smoking and cooking slots, where they dried in seconds, and only green leaves could be tied into a hand. They now stay out of those slots, and two bunches that have air-dried tie into a cured hand.
- Whole watermelons and cantaloupes have vanilla's pumpkin health (50, was 10).
- Melons open with every tool vanilla's pumpkin takes (hoe, shovel, crowbar, hammer, mace, wrenches, screwdriver, sledgehammer, bat, pickaxe), and the slices drop beside you like the pumpkin's instead of into your inventory, where a filtered container such as a Gebsfish tackle box would lose them at the next restart.
- A tobacco bale's condition is the average of the hands in it; adding a worn hand used to leave the bale as it was.
- Frozen tobacco leaves and hands can't be tied or baled, as vanilla's seed recipes refuse frozen food.
- Every crop, slice, seed packet, and the tobacco hand and bale, is held with the pose of the closest vanilla item (berries, tomato, pepper, zucchini, pumpkin, seed packs, firewood) instead of the generic one.
- Wild ginseng is sometimes found dried or rotten, as vanilla's mushrooms are; garden ginseng is still harvested fresh.
- Ginseng lasts an hour on the ground (was 15 minutes), so a garden harvest dropped at your feet doesn't vanish before you pick it up. The events file and the README note that ginseng shares the mushrooms' event, and how to keep as many mushrooms.

## 1.0.0

First release as its own mod. Geb's Flora & Fauna is now three mods, so a server can run the parts it wants: [Geb's Animals](https://github.com/PackJC/gebsanimals), **Geb's Plants** (this one) and [Geb's Mushrooms](https://github.com/PackJC/gebsmushrooms). Classnames are unchanged.

Server owners: the released Geb's Flora & Fauna had the animals and four mushrooms but no plants, so Geb's Plants only adds new items and a server can leave it out without anyone losing anything. To keep the animals and mushrooms players already have, a server that ran Flora & Fauna loads Geb's Animals and Geb's Mushrooms; leaving one of those out deletes its items from the server's storage at the next restart.

### Added
- **Ready-made economy files**, like Gebsfish: on start the server writes `gebsplants-types.xml`, `gebsplants-events-children.xml` and `gebsplants-cfgeconomycore.xml` to `$profile:Gebs/mpmissions/`, with the types for every crop, seed, seed packet and plant (packets spawn as farm loot) and the ginseng event line. They're only rewritten when the mod's version changes (or a file is deleted), so your edits stay put.
- **27 garden crops**, each grown from its own seed packet through five stages in a garden plot, to a harvest you can eat raw, bake, boil or dry (tobacco is cured instead): corn, okra, green beans, onions, garlic, yellow squash, watermelon, cantaloupe, carrots, cucumbers, barley, tobacco, cabbage, collards, sweet potatoes, beets, radishes, turnips, peas, pole beans, peanuts, strawberries, hops, sorghum, sunflowers, leeks and ginseng.
- **Seed saving:** cut, thresh or shell fresh seeds back out of a raw crop with a blade. Garlic splits into cloves and sweet potatoes into seed pieces; some crops come from packets only.
- Watermelons and cantaloupes are cut into slices with a blade, like vanilla's pumpkin.
- **Tobacco:** tie leaves into hands, cure them in the smoke of a stone oven, a closed barrel stove or an indoor fireplace, and bale them with rope.
- **Wild American ginseng** grows on the forest floor near players in shady hardwood coves, like vanilla's mushrooms. Chew the root, boil it into a tea, dry it to keep, or grow it from seed.
- Every string in all 13 languages: English, Czech, German, Russian, Polish, Hungarian, Italian, Spanish, French, Chinese (Traditional and Simplified), Japanese and Portuguese.
- Mod logo in the same style as Geb's other mods (a sprout, white in a black frame and green when hovered), and a website with every crop, the server setup and a searchable classname list: [packjc.github.io/gebsplants](https://packjc.github.io/gebsplants/).

### Fixed (since Geb's Flora & Fauna)
- **Melon slices follow the melon's condition.** Every slice took the whole melon's quantity, so a damaged or part-eaten melon still gave full slices; they're now smaller to match.
