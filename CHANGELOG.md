# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release as its own mod. Geb's Flora & Fauna is now three mods, so a server can run the parts it wants: [Geb's Animals](https://github.com/PackJC/gebsanimals), **Geb's Plants** (this one) and [Geb's Mushrooms](https://github.com/PackJC/gebsmushrooms). Classnames are unchanged.

Server owners: a server that ran Geb's Flora & Fauna should load all three to keep every item players already have. Leaving one out deletes its items from the server's storage at the next restart.

### Added
- **27 garden crops**, each grown from its own seed packet through five stages in a garden plot, to a harvest you can eat raw, bake, boil or dry: corn, okra, green beans, onions, garlic, yellow squash, watermelon, cantaloupe, carrots, cucumbers, barley, tobacco, cabbage, collards, sweet potatoes, beets, radishes, turnips, peas, pole beans, peanuts, strawberries, hops, sorghum, sunflowers, leeks and ginseng.
- **Seed saving:** cut, thresh or shell fresh seeds back out of a raw crop with a blade. Garlic splits into cloves and sweet potatoes into seed pieces; some crops come from packets only.
- Watermelons and cantaloupes are cut into slices with a blade, like vanilla's pumpkin.
- **Tobacco:** tie leaves into hands, cure them in the smoke of a stone oven, a closed barrel stove or an indoor fireplace, and bale them with rope.
- **Wild American ginseng** grows on the forest floor near players in shady hardwood coves, like vanilla's mushrooms. Chew the root, boil it into a tea, dry it to keep, or grow it from seed.
- Every string in all 13 languages: English, Czech, German, Russian, Polish, Hungarian, Italian, Spanish, French, Chinese (Traditional and Simplified), Japanese and Portuguese.
- Mod logo in the same style as Geb's other mods (a sprout, white in a black frame and green when hovered), and a website with every crop, the server setup and a searchable classname list: [packjc.github.io/gebsplants](https://packjc.github.io/gebsplants/).
