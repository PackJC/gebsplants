Gardening

Grow your own.

Thirty crops, each from its own seed packet, through five growth stages in a garden plot. Eat most harvests raw, bake them, boil them or dry them for later; tobacco is cured in the smoke instead, and cannabis buds are rolled into joints. Every plant and harvest is made from real photos, like vanilla's garden: the leaves sway in the wind, cooked strawberries, onions, garlic and radishes wrinkle and shrivel, and burnt food chars black and brown.

  1. Open a packet.Each crop comes in a Geb's Garden seed packet, found as loot.
  2. Plant and water.Sow in a tilled garden plot, just like vanilla seeds.
  3. Harvest.Pick it when ripe, before it dries out or spoils.

Wild plants

Forage the woods.

Wild plants grow on the forest floor near players, like vanilla's mushrooms. Dig them whole, eat or brew them, and keep the seeds for your garden.

For server owners

Set up in minutes.

Nothing replaces vanilla. Seed packets are ordinary farm loot and wild ginseng joins the vanilla forest-floor event, so you add only what you want.

  1. 1

    Install the mod

    Subscribe on the Workshop, place @gebsplants beside the server executable, copy its key into keys/, and load -mod=@gebsplants on the server. Every player needs the mod too.

  2. 2

    Start the server once

    The mod writes gebsplants-types.xml, gebsplants-events-children.xml and gebsplants-cfgeconomycore.xml into $profile:Gebs/mpmissions/, beside Gebsfish's. Each is stamped with the mod's version and only rewritten when that version changes, so your edits stay put until then; delete a file to have it written again.

  3. 3

    Register the types

    Copy gebsplants-types.xml into mpmissions/<your mission>/gebsplants/ and paste this block inside <economycore> in cfgeconomycore.xml.

    <ce folder="gebsplants">
    <file name="gebsplants-types.xml" type="types" />
</ce>
  4. 4

    Let wild ginseng grow

    Seed packets spawn as farm loot from the types file alone. For wild ginseng, merge the <child> line from gebsplants-events-children.xml into the vanilla event that scatters forage on the forest floor:

  5. 5

    Test it

    Stop the server before editing and keep backups. Validate the XML, restart, and read the RPT and script logs. Values that suit Chernarus may not suit Sakhal, Livonia or a custom map.

Help

Questions.

How do the garden crops grow?

Empty a Geb's Garden seed packet, plant the seeds in a tilled garden plot and water them, as with vanilla seeds. Each crop grows through five stages; harvest it when ripe, before it dries or spoils. Cut fresh seeds out of a raw harvest with any blade: thresh barley and sorghum, shell peas and peanuts, cut sweet potatoes into seed pieces, take ginseng seeds from the berries, and split garlic into cloves. Onions, carrots, cabbage, collards, beets, radishes, turnips, leeks, hops, opium poppies, coca and cannabis come from packets only. A whole watermelon or cantaloupe is too big to eat: prepare it with a blade for slices and seeds, as with vanilla's pumpkin. Peel a sweet potato with a blade before you eat or cook it, as with vanilla's potato.

Do the crops look like vanilla's?

Yes. Every plant is built the way vanilla builds its tomatoes, zucchini and potatoes: each leaf is a photo cut-out on a light card, with vanilla's swaying leaf materials, relief and shine, and ripe plants carry their crop as vanilla's zucchini does. The harvests come from free photos of the real crops, and every stage is cooked from those photos: raw, baked, boiled, dried, burnt and rotten. Cooked strawberries, onions, garlic and radishes wrinkle and shrivel like vanilla's cooked tomato and pepper, and burnt food is charred like vanilla's, mottled black and brown with grey ash, instead of a flat black.

Is there a guide in the game?

Yes: Geb's Garden Guide, a field guide to all 30 crops printed on a folded map, found on the shelves of farms, villages and towns. It covers when each crop ripens, what one plant gives and how to grow them.

Where do I find ginseng?

On the forest floor in hardwood woods, once the server adds it to the vanilla TrajectoryDeciduous event. You find the whole dug plant: look for three prongs of five toothed leaflets and a cluster of red berries over a forked, ringed tan root. Chew the root for a bitter-sweet lift, boil it into a tea, or dry it to keep and trade. You can also grow it: sow seeds from a Geb's Garden packet, or take them from the red berries with a blade, and dig the plants once they carry ripe berries.

How do I cure tobacco?

Combine partial bunches or tie two bunches into a hand, then add matching leaves or hands until it holds 300 g. Keep green and cured tobacco separate. Hang the hand in a smoking slot of a stone oven, a closed barrel stove or an indoor fireplace, and take it out when it turns golden brown, after about eight minutes of smoke; left in, it scorches. A hand left hanging anywhere else may cure by itself or rot. Start a bale with a cured hand and rope, then add cured hands, including partial ones, until it holds five full hands. Any excess stays in the hand you are adding.

How do I smoke cannabis?

Grow it from a Cannabis Seed Packet: a ripe plant gives three buds of 5 g, which stack in grams. Combine a bud with a sheet of paper to roll a joint (it takes a gram), then combine the joint with a lighter or a box of matches to light it. Hold the lit joint and hold left click to draw on it: six puffs to a joint, and now and then a cough. Every puff tints the world round the colours of the rainbow for two and a half minutes more, up to 25 minutes, and the first few puffs make it stronger. Only the smoker sees it. A mask gets in the way, as with eating; dying ends it, and it doesn't carry over a logout.

Where did the animals and mushrooms go?

Geb's Plants grew out of Geb's Flora & Fauna. It is now three mods, so servers can pick what they want: Geb's Animals has the wildlife, and Geb's Mushrooms has the 69 fungi. The classnames haven't changed. The released Flora & Fauna had the animals and four mushrooms but no plants, so Geb's Plants only adds new items: if your server used the old mod, load Geb's Animals and Geb's Mushrooms to keep everything players already have.

Does every player need the mod?

Yes. It supplies the models, textures, scripts and item names.

Can I put it in a server pack?

No. Use the Workshop item, and a Steam Collection for your server's required mods.
