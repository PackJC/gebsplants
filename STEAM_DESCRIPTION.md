[h1]Mod Information[/h1]
Geb's Plants brings gardening and foraging to DayZ. It adds [b]27 garden crops[/b], each grown from its own Geb's Garden seed packet, and [b]wild ginseng[/b] on the forest floor. Every harvest can be eaten raw, baked, boiled or dried.

It's meant to add immersion, not to make survival easier: crops take real time to grow, and seed packets are ordinary loot.

[b]Website (every crop, the setup guide and all the classnames):[/b] [url=https://packjc.github.io/gebsplants/]packjc.github.io/gebsplants[/url]

[h1]Requirements[/h1]
[list]
[*] DayZ 1.30.
[*] No other mods. Load it on both the server and every client.
[/list]

[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*] [b]27 garden crops:[/b] corn, okra, green beans, onions, garlic, yellow squash, watermelon, cantaloupe, carrots, cucumbers, barley, tobacco, cabbage, collards, sweet potatoes, beets, radishes, turnips, peas, pole beans, peanuts, strawberries, hops, sorghum, sunflowers, leeks and ginseng.
[*] [b]Real growing:[/b] sow the seeds from a packet in a tilled garden plot, water them, and watch five growth stages. Harvest when ripe, before it dries out or spoils.
[*] [b]Seed saving:[/b] cut, thresh or shell fresh seeds out of a raw harvest with a blade. Garlic splits into cloves and sweet potatoes into seed pieces; some crops come from packets only.
[*] [b]Melons:[/b] a whole watermelon or cantaloupe is cut into slices with a blade, like vanilla's pumpkin.
[*] [b]Tobacco:[/b] tie the leaves into hands, cure them in the smoke of a stone oven, a closed barrel stove or an indoor fireplace, and bale them with rope.
[*] [b]Wild ginseng ("sang"):[/b] dig it whole in shady hardwood woods. Chew the root, boil it into a tea or dry it to keep, and grow more from its berries.
[*] [b]Server-controlled by design:[/b] nothing replaces vanilla. Seed packets are loot and ginseng joins the vanilla forest-floor event, so you add only what you want.
[/list]

[h1]Server Setup[/h1]
[list]
[*] [b]Seed packets:[/b] copy vanilla's TomatoSeedsPack entry in [b]types.xml[/b] once for each packet and change the name.
[*] [b]Wild ginseng:[/b] give geb_Ginseng a types.xml entry like vanilla's AgaricusMushroom, and add it to the vanilla [b]TrajectoryDeciduous[/b] event's children.
[/list]
The website has the full list of packet classnames and copy-ready XML.

[h1]Coming from Geb's Flora & Fauna[/h1]
Geb's Plants used to be part of Geb's Flora & Fauna. It is now three mods, so servers can run the parts they want: [b]Geb's Animals[/b] (15 wildlife species), [b]Geb's Plants[/b] (this one) and [b]Geb's Mushrooms[/b] (63 wild fungi). The classnames haven't changed. If your server ran Geb's Flora & Fauna, load all three to keep the items players already have.
